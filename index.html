Instructions

For each statement, select the response that best describes your time after work.

Privacy: Your responses and results stay in this browser using local storage. Nothing is transmitted by this site.

Results

Each score is the mean of four responses and ranges from 1 to 5. Higher scores indicate more of that recovery experience.

Important: This questionnaire is intended for reflection, education, or research. It is not a clinical diagnostic tool and does not evaluate job performance.

Attribution: Recovery Experience Questionnaire developed by Sabine Sonnentag and Charlotte Fritz.
